Careers

Build research and products with us

We want to hire people who are curious, careful and keen to contribute to research, product development and new areas of health science. We have two research positions open.

Working with us

We are growing our research and product-development work. We would like to meet people who read the science closely, write clearly and enjoy working with others. You will work on questions that are still open, so we value good judgment and honesty about what the evidence shows.

Both positions below are research roles. Choose the one that fits you, or apply for both with two applications.

Research Associate – Peptide Research & Product Development

What you will do

  • Support our peptide-related research activities.
  • Research and analyze scientific literature.
  • Help with product development and product evaluation.
  • Document research activities.
  • Work with internal and external scientific partners.
  • Prepare research summaries and technical documents.

Who we are looking for

  • A background in biochemistry, biology, biotechnology, pharmacology, pharmaceutical sciences or a related field.
  • Strong research and analysis skills.
  • The ability to review and interpret scientific literature.
  • Good written communication and documentation skills.
  • An interest in peptide science and product development.

Apply for the Peptide Research position

Research Associate – Cellular Research & Longevity

What you will do

  • Support research into cell biology and aging-related processes.
  • Review scientific literature.
  • Document and analyze research.
  • Help assess emerging scientific approaches.
  • Contribute to research projects about longevity and wellness.
  • Work with scientific and external partners.

Who we are looking for

  • A background in cell biology, molecular biology, biotechnology, biomedical sciences or a related discipline.
  • Familiarity with scientific research methods.
  • Strong analytical and organizational skills.
  • An interest in cellular science, aging and longevity research.

Apply for the Cellular Research position
