About us

Who we are and how we work

We are a health and life-sciences company. We develop, source and distribute products, and we carry out research and development.

Our company

The Medics Inc. is a health and life-sciences company. Our activities cover three connected areas: research, product development and distribution.

Our business includes:

  • Peptides and peptide-related products.
  • CBD and hemp-derived products.
  • Products under our own brands.
  • Distribution of products from third-party manufacturers.
  • Wholesale and distribution to healthcare professionals.
  • Research and development in cellular science, longevity and healthy aging.

How our activities fit together

Our commercial activities give us practical experience with products, quality expectations and the needs of professional customers. Our research and development programs take new science from review and laboratory work toward clinical studies. The two support each other, but we do not mix them up.

When we describe something we sell, we say so. When we describe something we are developing, we say which stage it has reached. We do not describe a product in development as approved or proven in people until the evidence supports it.

Commercial activity or research and development?Peptide products and CBD and hemp-derived products are part of our established commercial activity. Cellular research, longevity and healthy aging are our research and development programs.

Our history

We previously operated in New Jersey’s regulated medical cannabis sector. As that market changed with the move to adult-use legalization, we shifted our focus. We kept our hemp and CBD business, which remains an important part of what we do. Today we also work in peptides, distribution to healthcare professionals, and research and development.

You can follow us on X, formerly Twitter (opens in a new tab).

Our business model

We do three things. We develop and sell products under our own brands. We distribute products made by other manufacturers. We supply wholesale customers, including healthcare professionals. Our research and development work supports our product development and helps us decide where to look next.

Our philosophy

  • We are careful with claims. We say only what we can support. We do not promise that a product reverses aging, cures a disease or produces a clinically proven result.
  • We respect the role of professionals. Healthcare professionals make their own decisions about their patients. Our information does not replace their judgment.
  • We are open about where we stand. We tell you what is established, what is in development and what is still a question.
  • We treat people’s information with care. We collect only what we need, and we explain how we use it.

Where we work

Our headquarters is at 100 Challenger Rd in Ridgefield Park, New Jersey. Our Miami R&D Center is at 1951 NW 7th Ave in Miami, Florida. The Miami site supports our research and development work. It is not our headquarters. Our office at 1441 Canal St in New Orleans, Louisiana, is a further place where we work. Read more about our locations.

What we are not

We are not a medical clinic. We do not examine, diagnose or treat patients, and we do not offer medical care. Our website is for general information. Please read our Health and Product Disclaimer.

Our compliance approach

We explain our privacy, security and regulatory position in plain language, and we do not claim certifications we do not hold. You can read the details on our compliance and regulatory page.
