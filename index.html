We are The Medics Inc. Our work covers peptide products, hemp-derived and CBD products, and distribution to healthcare professionals. We also carry out research and development in cellular science, longevity and healthy aging.

About our company Our research and development

Who we are

We bring products, distribution and research together.

We make and source health-related products, and we distribute them to healthcare professionals and other trade customers. Next to that work, we invest in research and development. We want to learn how new science may lead to better products in the future.

We keep these two parts of our work clearly apart on this website. What we sell today is described as products. What we are developing is described as research and development. We do not claim that our products prevent, treat or cure disease, and we do not claim clinical results that have not been proven.
